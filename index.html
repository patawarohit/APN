FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to questions we hear most often.

Who needs to file an Income Tax Return (ITR)?
Any individual with gross income exceeding the basic exemption limit (₹2.5L / ₹3L for seniors), as well as businesses, LLPs, and companies, must file an ITR annually.
What documents are required for GST registration?
PAN of the entity, Aadhaar of proprietor/directors, proof of business address (rent agreement/electricity bill), bank account details, and photographs are typically required.
How long does company incorporation take?
With all documents in order, a Private Limited Company can be incorporated within 7–10 working days on average. We handle the entire process end-to-end.
What are your professional fees?
Our fees are transparent and competitive, based on the complexity of work. Contact us for a customised quote — we offer free initial consultations to all prospective clients.
Do you offer services online / remotely?
Absolutely! We serve clients across India online. Documents can be shared securely via WhatsApp or email, and consultations are available via video call.
Can you help with tax-saving investment planning?
Yes! Our financial planning service covers 80C/80D deductions, ELSS, PPF, NPS, and other instruments to legally minimise your tax liability while growing your wealth.
